How to read our photos (4 standards)
  1. Photos are taken under consistent lighting, camera distance, and neutral expression wherever possible.
  2. Clinical photographs are not cosmetically retouched to alter bone contour or skin texture.
  3. Each set notes the recovery stage (before, early post-op, or stabilised follow-up), because swelling changes appearance over months.
